Compiled from open-source reporting: Darfur24, Radio Dabanga, Radio Tamazuj, Al Jazeera, Anadolu, Asharq Al-Awsat, AP, UN News, MSF and WHO. Each incident links to its main source.

Scope and classes: critical infrastructure covers power stations, substations and dams; fuel depots, fuel stations and oil facilities; water sources and stations; hospitals, clinics and medical stores; airports and railways; and humanitarian facilities and warehouses. Markets covers general markets and fuel markets. Aid and commercial convoys covers aid trucks, convoys and commercial fuel or goods vehicles. Strikes on homes, factories, mines and shelters are left out. Where one strike hit both a market and a hospital or water source, it is counted under critical infrastructure.

This is a log of reported incidents, not a census: official tallies are higher, and many strikes are reported without naming the facility hit. Casualty figures and attributions are as reported and unverified; blank deaths mean no figure was given. Map positions are approximate, to town level.
